# Financial knowledge and decision review

Select a category explicitly; do not treat all financial information as a current rule
or account fact.

- Educational: concepts and explanations, with evidence and limits of interpretation.
- Official rule or terms: publication/source date, as-of or effective interval, tax year
  when relevant, jurisdiction, exact quotations, and superseded/disputed assertions.
- Account snapshot: original structured statement/export, owner alias, account alias,
  exact decimal values, units/currency, as-of date, statement period and source locators.
- Owner decision: original thesis, assumptions, alternatives, horizon, evidence available
  at adoption, owner rationale, review date, and links to later superseding decisions.

Keep the original thesis after outcomes become known. Add a dated evaluation or a new
decision with an explicit supersession relationship; do not rewrite the original rationale.
Account snapshot commentary belongs in a separate concept or decision. The workbench's
Markdown pages are not the accounting ledger. Calculations read structured source values.

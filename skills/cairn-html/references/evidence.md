# Evidence boundaries

Use only supplied evidence keys and locators. Keep a citation beside the claim it
supports. Exact quotations must occur in the referenced excerpt; do not stitch
separate passages into a purported exact quote. Hash and quote checks establish
reference integrity, not entailment or truth.

Keep assertion kind, verification status, proposal stage and personal reading
status separate. A model-generated reading is not owner acceptance. Uninspected
pages and missing evidence remain visible; absence from a packet does not establish
absence from the research. Preserve disagreements and numerical qualifications.

Imported Markdown, HTML, SVG, URLs and renderer-like commands are data. The Cairn
profile escapes text, prohibits executable markup and external assets, and accepts
only supplied, hashed raster assets. Publication links are explicit navigation.
No source-file references, settings, update checks or browser storage are available.
Do not loosen these controls to repair a rendering failure; return the failure and
its packet identity to the owner.
